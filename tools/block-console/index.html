The originator and the position owner are always blocked. The receiver is only blocked when you tick this — it is never an executor, so blocking it stops the stuck-exit recovery leg as well.

Before you generate.
  • The batch is atomic on chain: one unknown id reverts the whole call. Only ids read from this page’s list can be selected, which is what keeps that from happening.
  • A freeze holds funds; a blacklist is the determination that this is theft and is the only state that unlocks recovery. A frozen address escalates to blacklisted in place; a blacklisted one never silently downgrades.
  • Neither lever stops a withdrawal being initiated — exits keep escrowing. What stops is the payout at the far end of the hold.

The levers are gated to the queue’s Admin or Owner. At launch that is the Exchequer Multisig, so the generated calldata is submitted there and confirmed by its owners. Three of the seven must act: one submits, two more confirm — and the third confirmation executes the call in the same transaction. There is no separate execution step.

Submit — the first owner

  1. Open the multisig on Blockscout and connect your wallet. Blockscout connects browser and hardware wallets directly; no other app is needed.
  2. Find the method: type submitTransaction into “Search by method name”. The list collapses to 20. submitTransaction. Click the row to expand it.
  3. Fill the three fields from the Action tab:
    destination — the queue address.
    value — 0. Leave the ×10¹⁸ multiplier untouched.
    data — the generated hex, one line, starting 0x.
  4. Press Simulate first. It must succeed. A failed simulation means a mis-pasted field or a stale selection — do not submit; regenerate from a fresh load.
  5. Press Write and approve in your wallet.
  6. Read the transaction id and share it. Your transaction’s Logs tab shows a Submission event whose transactionId is what the other owners need. Your submission already counts as the first confirmation.

Confirm — the second and third owners

  1. Open the same page and connect your wallet.
  2. Search for confirmTransaction — method 4. confirmTransaction — and expand it.
  3. Enter the shared transactionId, Simulate, then Write.
  4. The third confirmation executes the call in the same transaction. A noticeably higher gas estimate on that one is expected — it is the one carrying the execution.

Verify

  • Method 12. isConfirmed with the id returns true once the threshold is reached.
  • Reload this console: the blocked parties should show their new state in the Parties blocked column.
Three rules that do not bend.
  • value is always 0. A prepared Perimeter transaction never asks the multisig to send RBTC. If one does, stop and raise it.
  • Only submit calldata you generated here, or that came through the agreed channel. The hex is opaque on its face; its meaning comes from the preview that accompanies it.
  • Wrong submission? Before the threshold is reached, any owner who confirmed can withdraw their confirmation with method 19. revokeConfirmation. A transaction that never reaches three confirmations never executes.
